# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-20

- Mobile-first iPhone PWA shell with offline-capable static asset caching.
- Work/personal manual odometer entries, unfinished morning entries, hours worked, truck billable hours and job descriptions.
- Company-specific kilometre, truck-hour and labour rates; fuel surcharge methods and per-trip charge toggles.
- Revenue/cost dashboards and date filters.
- Receipt-backed vehicle expense logging.
- Full backup export and restore, plus CSV audit package and local change history.

Known next milestones: iPhone Safari acceptance testing, mileage overlap/gap detection, receipt preview refinements, optional private sync.
